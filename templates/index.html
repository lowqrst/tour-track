Behind the score / 02

Every journey
has a rhythm.

Our activity score brings together tour reach, listed concerts, and years of making music. Simple rules. A fresh perspective.

Countries × 2+Concerts × 3+Career bonus up to 10
Emerging0–10 points
Active11–25 points
Established26–40 points
Legendary41+ points
A closer look at the calculation +

Score = (unique tour countries × 2) + (listed concert dates × 3) + min(10, career years ÷ 5). Career years use the current server year; the career bonus uses whole-number division.

A Rising Star has a concert rate over the last two years more than 50% above their career average. Future and invalid dates are excluded from that recent window. These labels describe this API collection, not an artist’s popularity or current touring status.
